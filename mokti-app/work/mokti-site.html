The job

Mokti began as a Shopify store. I rebuilt it as a hand-coded website so that my services, ebooks, AI guides and writing could live in one place that I fully control.

It had to take payments, deliver ebooks without me doing it by hand, and collect project enquiries.

What I did

  • Designed the warm ivory and gold look, including the 3D book covers and layered panels
  • Wrote and built every page by hand, without a template or page builder
  • Connected a Razorpay Payment Page to each of the 19 ebooks and bundles
  • Wrote a Netlify Function that receives each Razorpay payment and emails the right PDF through Resend
  • Set up Netlify Forms for project enquiries, contact and reader submissions
  • Redirected the old Shopify links so existing links keep working

Features you can see

  • Services with clear packages, and an enquiry form that fills in the package you clicked
  • AI guides and books with instant checkout
  • Automatic ebook delivery by email after payment
  • A blog I write and publish myself from a simple editor at /admin
  • Works on phones, and turns off motion for people who prefer less of it

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework. Hosted on Netlify with Netlify Functions and Netlify Forms. Payments by Razorpay, email by Resend.
