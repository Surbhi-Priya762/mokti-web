Shopify

Storeily Shopify store

Day-to-day running of a Shopify fashion store: orders, products, collections and analytics.

Role
Store operations
Platform
Shopify

The job

Storeily sells T-shirts and fashion on Shopify. A catalogue of hundreds of products has to stay accurate and well organised so shoppers can find things, while orders keep moving every day.

What I did

  • Ran the store day to day: orders, products and store setup
  • Created product listings with titles, pricing, images and stock, including AI-assisted bulk listing
  • Built the collection structure, including seasonal launches such as Holi, Valentine’s Day and Pet Lover
  • Tracked sales, sessions and conversion to decide what to promote next

Features you can see

  • Automatic collections driven by product tags, for example every product tagged “holi” joins the Holi collection
  • Collections for women’s T-shirts, best sellers, limited editions and themed days such as World Environment Day and Yoga Day
  • A product catalogue with prices, images and stock in one list
  • Store analytics for sales, sessions and conversion

Built with

Shopify admin, Shopify analytics and AI tools for bulk product listing.
