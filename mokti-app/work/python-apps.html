Python apps

Mood quiz & campaign analyzer

Two small Python apps built with Streamlit and published online, so anyone can try them in a browser.

Role
Idea, build and publishing
Tools
Python, Streamlit

Try the mood quiz →Try the campaign analyzer →

The job

Mood quiz: help a shopper find a Mokti product by how they feel, instead of scrolling a catalogue.

Campaign analyzer: let a small seller check whether an ad campaign paid off without building a spreadsheet.

What I did

  • Wrote both apps in Python with Streamlit
  • Designed the quiz questions and matched each mood to a product
  • Published both apps on Streamlit so they open in any browser

Features you can see

  • Mood quiz: choose from four moods, such as “Chill calm days” or “Lost deep in stories”, and see a matching product with its picture
  • Campaign analyzer: enter ad spend, clicks, orders and revenue to see how the campaign performed, including ROI

Built with

Python and Streamlit, hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud.
