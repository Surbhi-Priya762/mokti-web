About

Hi, I'm Surbhi Priya. I work where data meets creativity: I run ecommerce operations by day, and I write love stories and build things for small businesses the rest of the time.

I'm based in Bangalore and have spent 7+ years in D2C ecommerce, analytics and design. Mokti is where all of it comes together.

My story

My day job is running backend operations and finance for two clothing brands, Lõma and Sauca: orders, listings, stock, pop-ups and month-end reporting across Shopify, Myntra, Nykaa and Coyu. I also designed and built the internal operations system both brands use. Before that, I spent 3+ years running D2C operations on Shopify, Myntra, Amazon and Flipkart, where I onboarded 300+ vendors, automated product listings with AI, and built dashboards that drive real decisions.

But the truest version of me is a storyteller. I've published three novels on Kindle, I make content on Instagram, and I built Mokti, a personal brand about freedom, self-expression and choosing yourself. Here you can read my stories and hire me for the same work I do every day.

What I offer

  • Ebooks — love stories, relationship guides and AI guides, written in simple Hindi-English
  • Websites and apps — websites built with AI, Shopify stores, and custom business apps like the production system I built for two clothing brands
  • Ecommerce — product listing and marketplace management on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa and Meesho, plus AI product mockups
  • Design and data — logos, Canva social media content, and Excel and Power BI dashboards
