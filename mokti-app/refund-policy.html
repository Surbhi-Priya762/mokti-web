Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Digital products (ebook, stories, chapters)

Because ebooks, stories and chapters are delivered instantly as downloadable PDF files, all sales are final and we do not offer refunds or cancellations once the download link has been sent. We will give a full refund if: you paid but did not receive the download link and we can't resolve it within 2 working days of you contacting us; the file is broken or can't be opened and we can't send a working copy; or you were charged more than once for the same order.

Services (websites, ecommerce, design and dashboards)

You can cancel a service project at any time by writing to us. If you cancel before work has started, you get a full refund of any advance paid. Once work has started, the advance is non-refundable, as it covers time already spent. If you cancel partway through, any amount paid beyond the advance is refunded in proportion to the work not yet done. No refunds are given after final files are delivered or the site goes live.

How to ask for a refund

Write to us through the Contact page with your name, the email used to pay, and the Razorpay payment ID. Please ask within 7 days of your payment. You can also email surbhi7priya@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 83173 69573. We reply within 2 working days.

When you'll get your money back

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Once approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
