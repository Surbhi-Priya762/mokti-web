Try a website before you buy one

Three sample websites for a salon, a boutique and a clinic. Watch each one in action, then tap it to try it yourself. The businesses are fictional, but every button, form and filter works, so you can see exactly what your customers would see. Your own site starts from one of these and is made yours: your name, photos, prices and colours.
