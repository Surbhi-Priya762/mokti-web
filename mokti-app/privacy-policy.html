Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information mokti.in collects and how it is used. The site is run by Mokti, run by Surbhi Priya (sole proprietor), Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

What we collect

  • Journal entries and quotes are saved only in your own browser on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Forms (the "You" page and the Contact page) send what you type, such as your name, contact details and message, to us through Netlify Forms. You can post on the "You" page anonymously.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive your name, email, phone and payment status, but never your card or bank details.
  • Cookies: we don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting and payment providers may set essential cookies needed for the site and checkout to work.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your messages, deliver what you bought and send your download link, carry out service projects, handle refunds and support, keep records the law requires, and, only with your consent, publish your story under Experience. We don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Who we share it with

We use Netlify (hosting and forms) and Razorpay (payments). They process your information only to provide their services to us. We may also share information if the law requires it. We never sell or rent your personal data.

How long we keep it

Form submissions and messages are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted. Order and payment records are kept for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law. Published stories stay up until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete the information you've sent us by writing through the Contact page. We reply within 2 working days and act on your request within 30 days.
