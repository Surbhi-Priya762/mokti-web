FAQ

Before you come in

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes. Patients with appointments are seen first, so walk-ins may wait a little longer.

What should I bring?

Your old prescriptions and reports, a list of medicines you take, and an ID for lab tests.

How do I pay?

Cash, card or UPI at the front desk.

Do you do home visits?

For elderly patients nearby, on request. Call the front desk to arrange one.
